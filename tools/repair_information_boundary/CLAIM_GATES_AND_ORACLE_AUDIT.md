## Noncompensatory promotion rules
LIRA/JEV remain task prioritization aids, not evidence grades. Claim-specific gates use PASS/FAIL/OPEN/NOT_APPLICABLE, no averaging; a missing required gate blocks promotion. Source-byte verification and finite protected observations permit only bounded implementation claims. General predictive structural-law claims require (1) independently disclosed task cohort, (2) source/contract/authority comparability, (3) equal-information strong rival frozen before outcomes and outperformed under predeclared metric, (4) independent outcome adjudication, (5) cost measurement matching claimed quantity and repository-level clustering, (6) replication beyond chosen case family. Human label necessary for human-judgment claims, not for purely mechanical exact tests. At present independent cohort, predictive superiority, external adjudication and replication OPEN.
