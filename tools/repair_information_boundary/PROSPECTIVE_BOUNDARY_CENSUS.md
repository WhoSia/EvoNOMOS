## Literature-driven scope amendment — DRAFT, not activated (2026-11-27)

Separate private worktree operations from observable integration and release checkpoints. Freeze the observer/exposure map and checkpoint policy before demand disclosure; a private noncompiling worktree is not automatically an old-client regression, while an invalid published build is. If the task explicitly requires continuously compiling private states, that is a separate operational contract, applied equally to both arms. Never remove failing checkpoint observations after the fact. Capture private edit/test/undo/rework events and costs even where not subject to external behavioral contracts. Compare complete lifecycle costs, including upfront pair construction, normalization, tools, source discovery, and checkpoint validation; record human effort only when measured. Eilertsen & Murphy (2024), DOI 10.1002/smr.2378, observed Local, Structure, Execute strategies and noncompiling intermediate worktrees; the paper does not validate this protocol or establish which strategy is superior. Prechelt et al. (2001), Vokac et al. (2004), and Arisholm & Sjoberg (2000) already provide substantial prior art on conditional pattern effects, human-task correlations and direct effort benchmarking.

For each arm, distinguish source/authority extraction, bounded semantic verification, repair search given a graph, and observed engineering execution. Classical guarded graph search, source/structure and historical co-change baselines, and configuration-only/extension/local/structural candidates must receive the same admissible data and budget. Source extraction and candidate-generation cost must not be charged only to the rival or hidden as a free oracle. Cai et al. (2019), DOI 10.1109/TSE.2018.2797899, is a strong architecture/dependency+history baseline, not a causal repair-effort oracle. CCR 2.0, Iris, Simuliris and DimSum are strong compositional semantics competitors; runtime/ghost resource ownership is not equivalent to authenticated repository modification rights. No general novelty claim from proof obligations, finite graph equality, or literature review.
