## A bounded mathematical bridge (standard theory, not originality)

For a *finite deterministic* set of concrete **OO design-realization states** S and a **declared set of typed maintenance changes** D, assume total transitions \`δ_d:S→S\`. The complete observation \`o:S→Y\` must specify which user-visible correctness, noninterference, and lifecycle effects are being claimed. Do not silently substitute a Q-only observable for S/L/C/A/Q.

An observational compression \`π:S→Z\` is valid at the current instant if equal π-values imply equal o-values. But this says nothing about whether a later maintenance operation acts unambiguously on the compressed state.

**Proposition 1 (quotient transition well-definedness).** The maps \`δ̄_d([s])=[δ_d(s)]\` exist unambiguously for every d iff equivalence classes are preserved by each δ_d:

\`s ~ t ⇒ δ_d(s) ~ δ_d(t)\` for every d.

Necessity: both representatives must yield the same quotient successor. Sufficiency: representative choice then cannot affect δ̄_d. This is a standard congruence/right-invariance argument.

**Proposition 2 (the canonical demand-trace quotient).** Define

\`s ≡_D t iff for every finite typed demand word w∈D*, o(δ_w(s))=o(δ_w(t))\`.

Then ≡_D is the **coarsest observation-respecting congruence** on S. Proof: equality for empty word forces equal observations; prepending each demand preserves indistinguishability; conversely any observation-respecting congruence implies identical outputs after every demand word by induction. Therefore the quotient transition machine is canonical **up to output-/demand-preserving isomorphism**. It is not canonical across changes to the declared demand alphabet D or the observation semantics o.

**Proposition 3 (finite construction).** Initialize \`P_0\` by equal observation. Refine with signatures \`(o(s), [δ_d(s)]_{P_k}:d∈D)\`. By induction, P_k identifies states with equal observations for every demand word of length at most k. Finite refinements strictly raise the number of classes and eventually stabilize after at most |S|−|P_0| strict refinements. Stable P is the quotient in Proposition 2. This is classical automata partition refinement (Moore/Myhill–Nerode-like), **not a novel EvoNOMOS mathematical theorem**.

**Implication for SOLID.** A label/score that identifies two designs in the same π-fiber is insufficient to predict all future demand traces whenever ≡_D separates them. A claimed SOLID *conditional projection* must preserve the action/output decision on the admitted subdomain; it need not be a structural isomorphism. A non-isomorphic OO architecture can be observationally equivalent under one finite requirement alphabet without being interchangeable under every conceivable maintenance demand.

## How a real explanatory paper could use this

1. Derive concrete, fully functionality-preserving OO transition signatures from *paired* maintenance histories, with complete externally tested requirements. Distinguish implementation choices: a demand generally induces a **set of possible successor implementations**, not one deterministic transition. The finite deterministic theorem is merely a fixed-treatment approximation. A realistic nondeterministic model needs a relation, policy-relative observation, and stronger bisimulation/game-style invariance; no such theorem or source mapping is asserted here.
2. Define explicit, preregistered *admissible demand alphabets* and observations, including typed noninterference and S/L/C/A/Q, plus program execution semantics for LSP admissibility.
3. Obtain a counterexample where simple static topological, interface-degree, Parnas anticipated change, Cai DRSpaces and historical semantic/cochange predictors assign the same projection but a **pre-outcome obligation-sensitive architecture variable** separates two future demand outcomes.
4. Measure prospective prediction and calibration on source-grounded held-out real OO requirements. A mathematically valid quotient plus fabricated toy traces is only a way to organize falsifiers, not scientific evidence.
5. Only after empirical identification consider a formal result showing a specific named SOLID guideline is a restriction/projection of the learned richer structure on a bounded region and ceases to be sound outside it.
