# G8 LAW-R1-P31 — Founding Explanandum Return and Structural-Ontology Reorientation

## Status and reason for correction

**AUTHORITATIVE DIRECTIONALITY CORRECTION — P31 remains OPEN.** This is neither a new empirical result nor a retrospective rewrite of P30/P31 hosted receipts. The project owner's correction is that EvoNOMOS was founded to investigate **a deeper explanatory structure of object-oriented software organization** from which SOLID-like principles might follow **as contingent special cases**, not to axiomatize SOLID and not to substitute general state-sufficiency theory for software-structure discovery.

The EvoNOMOS Notion Lab's *Founding Question* asks whether software design principles can be replaced by **conditional structural interventions** with learned effects, uncertainty, failure boundaries and sign reversals across environments and objectives. Its *HOT Constitution* states **EXPLANANDUM_CONSERVATIVE / METHODS_EVOLUTIONARY** and requires the *External Consequence Test*: each mainline advance must sharpen a structural action, observable condition, result vector, effect-sign prediction, failure boundary, abstention or actual engineering decision.

This correction is **not** a fresh LAW-R2 authorization or an approval to treat an elegant theorem as discovered object-oriented ontology.

## 1. What was displaced

P30 zlib/decoder/SQLite showed bounded current- and predictive-state insufficiency and prospective history-coordinate repair. P31 Phase 0 proved, on an exact-source disposable SQLite grid, that an environment-conditioned one-bit policy chooses between NOOP and RESERVE with fewer unnecessary write transactions while meeting a threshold. The result is valid **within its fixed tested grain**.

However, neither a decoder hidden buffer nor a SQLite allocator sequence is, by itself, a structural intervention on **object responsibilities, modular boundaries, interfaces, dependency direction or change propagation**. A correct predictive-state experiment is not automatically a candidate law of object-oriented architecture. The preceding P31 literature framing around CEGAR/action-sufficient representations made a **method** into the principal research object.

**Classification:** Existing SQLite state-policy and Swamp identity-history experiments are retained as **AUXILIARY_MECHANISM_OR_METHOD_EVIDENCE**. They do not satisfy the P31 mainline OBJECT_ORIENTED_STRUCTURAL_LAW_GATE unless a fresh, pre-outcome source-faithful architectural action and lifecycle consequence are demonstrated. In particular, Swamp application-ID migration can be a real engineering identity decision without being evidence for the desired OOP structure ontology.

## 2. Restored scientific object

Not “is the current state representation sufficient?” as an end in itself, but:

> Under an actual stream of changing requirements, what configuration of responsibilities, authority, knowledge exposure, dependency direction and capability obligations makes a particular object-oriented organization cheaper, safer or more adaptable; when does that advantage reverse, and can established SOLID rules be recovered as conditional consequences rather than assumed axioms?

The unit of explanation is a **source-grounded structural mechanism interacting with a demand sequence**:
- Structural geometry: module/class/interface boundaries, responsibility ownership, authority localization, client-to-provider coupling, dependency direction and capability bundles.
- Exogenous moderators: change locality/co-occurrence, frequency and asymmetry of changes, client fanout, anticipated provider volatility, semantic compatibility and constraints on truthful implementation.
- Rival structural interventions: e.g. direct dependency versus injected capability boundary; broad common interface versus split consumer-specific capabilities; distributed membership knowledge versus single authority; eager abstraction versus local direct implementation.
- Concrete observed consequence: initial implementation and later change propagation in executable code, using **distinct, noncollapsed S/L/C/A/Q lifecycle coordinates**, functionality and adversarial requirements.
- Scientific output: a **conditional law candidate** with signed/vector effect, moderator support, a prospective falsifier, a reversal boundary and an abstention region. No universal principle certification.

Do **not** conflate the ability to express a candidate in mathematical notation with having discovered a more fundamental ontology. A new primitive is acceptable only if it explains actual differences between competing structural interventions and their change trajectories that a less ambitious model cannot distinguish.

## 4. Immediate P31 scientific re-entry

**Mainline gate 0 — Real OOP structure.** A proposal must change or compare an actual object/interface/module responsibility boundary, ownership rule, capability contract or dependency edge. An SQL allocation policy, generic finite automaton or diagnostic state bit alone fails the gate.

**Gate 1 — Competition.** Freeze at least two feasible, functionality-comparable object-oriented realizations of **the same present demand**, e.g. \`DIRECT\` versus \`INVERT\` or \`BUNDLE\` versus \`SPLIT\`. Specify what commits, interfaces and tests each action entails.

**Gate 2 — Evolution.** Freeze at least one initial demand and a fresh follow-up change that redistributes work over the actual architecture. Demand-specific tests must be runnable. Do not infer follow-up cost from architecture diagrams alone.

**Gate 3 — Structural mechanism and moderators.** Freeze measurements of who possesses variation knowledge, who must change when one requirement changes, what clients require of each interface, and what cross-boundary propagation or conformance obligations the rival creates. Include conditions in which the opposite rival is expected to win.

**Gate 4 — Distinct lifecycle outcomes.** Report the established S/L/C/A/Q axes without collapsing them into one uncalibrated “good code” score. Surface, source churn and capability burden can disagree. Record correctness, failures and exact structural diffs.

**Gate 5 — Explanatory and generative test.** Compare competing mechanism-level explanations: does a candidate modifier predict actual changes in the sign/vector effect when transported to a **different maintenance demand or topology**? An original discovery must improve such predictions, not only redescribe measured tradeoffs.

**Gate 6 — SOLID-as-special-case test.** Only after the structure/effect mechanism survives: exhibit the assumptions that recover a familiar SOLID recommendation, and explicitly construct a valid world where relaxing an assumption makes the recommendation neutral or reversed. SOLID is an output of the explanatory theory, not a premise.

**Gate 7 — Claim ceiling.** A passed local demand pair is a bounded structural witness. It cannot itself establish the universal ontology, cross-repository generalization, LAW-R2, manuscript originality or general SOLID derivation.

## 5. The most promising next concrete experiment

Return to an **object-oriented dependency/capability choice with measured change propagation**:
- Contrast \`DIRECT\` and \`INVERT\` under frozen initial and follow-up provider change, with a modifier for how often providers vary versus how much adapter overhead exists. Use source-backed classes/interfaces and tests, not SQL ID policies.
- Contrast \`BUNDLE\` and \`SPLIT\` where consumer capabilities are disjoint versus truly co-required. Measure introduction cost, conformance obligations, downstream change surface and correctness; predeclare the expected sign reversal.
- Competing structural hypotheses:
  1. **Authority/variation localization:** concentrated change authority changes the blast radius of demand-propagated edits, but its initial construction burden can dominate when reuse does not recur.
  2. **Capability-obligation fit:** segmentation helps when the shared boundary obliges clients to implement extraneous behavior, but may lose when demand genuinely requires every capability together.

These are **existing bounded CIL-C1/CBL-C1 family directions**, not yet asserted new laws. Originality, if any, would have to come from a **new mechanism, discriminator or cross-demand signed-vector prediction**, demonstrated against serious competing explanations and real repositories.
