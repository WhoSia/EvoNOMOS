# LAW-R1-P5 — Generalized Conditional Design-Law Kernel Result

**Authority:** P5 bounded theory promotion; not a universal software-design theory.

## Outcome-driven update

The pre-outcome candidate `L=(X,A,Y,M,F,Π,U)` survives P5, but for a different reason than the strongest opening hypothesis.

The three-interface pilot did **not** show a robust general maxim prior. Stronger interfaces often used named principles correctly as compressed cues while still reversing or abstaining on non-applicable cells. Only GPT-OSS 20B showed a modest named-principle pull, concentrated in DIP/OCP reversal cells.

Therefore the theory should not be built around “debiasing SOLID.” It should be built around **separating principle recognition from principle authority**.

### Principle recognition
A named principle may efficiently nominate a plausible structural rival.

### Principle authority
Whether that rival deserves testing or recommendation must be earned from moderators, predicted outcome channels, falsifiers and world contact.

These are different epistemic roles.

## Working kernel

[
\mathcal L=(\mathcal X,\mathcal A,\mathbf Y,M,F,\Pi,U)
]

The most important P5 addition is an action-space constraint:

[
\mathrm{ABSTAIN}\not\equiv a_i\quad\forall a_i\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{intervention}}
]

`ABSTAIN` is not “keep the current design” as just another rival implementation. It means **no structural intervention has earned authority from the available evidence**. Any status-quo-preserving intervention encoded inside the rival set must still be a genuine counterfactual action distinct from non-intervention.

## SOLID embedding

- SRP: generator for partition interventions under separable change causes.
- OCP: generator for extension-boundary interventions under repeated variants + stable core.
- LSP: admissibility/substitutability constraint over candidate subtype interventions.
- ISP: generator for capability partition under demand-extraneous conformance burden.
- DIP: generator for dependency-direction/policy-boundary interventions under volatile implementations behind stable semantics.

A principle can be useful even when it is not authoritative.

## P5 empirical lesson

For high-capability interfaces in this pilot, named principle information was often neutral or helpful. Hence a future EvoNOMOS decision service should not hide or ban SOLID names. It should compile them into **candidate hypotheses**, then require moderator/falsifier checks before authority is granted.

This makes the replacement target more general than SOLID without pretending SOLID is obsolete:

`MAXIM -> GENERATOR -> CONDITIONAL TEST -> WORLD CONTACT -> UPDATE / REVERSE / ABSTAIN`.

## Remaining novelty risk

Prior art already owns large portions of this structure individually. A strong novelty claim requires a dedicated synthesis court showing that no earlier framework already joins:
1. principle/tactic generators,
2. moderator-conditioned rivals,
3. observed non-scalar outcome vectors,
4. sign reversals and failure boundaries,
5. explicit no-intervention abstention,
6. prospective empirical authority update/revocation.

Until then, call this a **candidate kernel**, not a new general law of software design.
