# Equal-information competing explanations

Rival 1: ordinary contextual / observational equivalence over the declared probe set.

Rival 2: finite labeled transition systems and reachability under authorized edits.

Rival 3: alias/effect and separation-logic style noninterference.

Rival 4: architecture-independent exhaustive source edit search with equal rights and budget.

Rival 5: standard software maintenance studies with experience, task difficulty and familiarity as moderators.

The goal is not to defeat an omniscient exhaustive search but to establish reliable compression, transfer or explanation at controlled computational and evidence budgets.
