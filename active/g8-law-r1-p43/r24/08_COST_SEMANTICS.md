# Cost semantics

Keep distinct: (1) application-owned edit count, (2) changed source lines, (3) files/modules, (4) compilation/tests, (5) runtime regressions, (6) human engineering time, (7) reviewer/authorization burden.

A minimum edit-count advantage does not imply an engineering-time advantage.

Do not collapse a vector of costs to one number without an explicit stakeholder preference or justified weighting.

Use Pareto frontiers where tradeoffs occur; infinity means no path in the declared grammar, not universal impossibility.
