# What EAC could add beyond SOLID (not yet an established discovery)

SOLID is useful as a family of design heuristics concerning responsibility, extension, substitution, interfaces and dependencies. It is not itself a predictive quantitative theory for arbitrary future maintenance.

EAC is a *candidate verification protocol* grounded in:
- externally specified customer observations;
- source-level edit rights and effects;
- preservation at every intermediate change;
- future demand realization;
- honest cost and evidence semantics;
- matched competitors.

What it potentially adds: falsifiable, source-indexed claims and explicit countermodels where a broad design slogan is insufficient.

What it does NOT add yet: a non-classical theorem, empirical improvement in human maintenance, a complete source extraction algorithm or a universal axiom set superior to SOLID. We should not call it a replacement theory until independently verified predictive, explanatory or proof-compression advantage is demonstrated.
