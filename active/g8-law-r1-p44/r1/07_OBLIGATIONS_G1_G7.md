G2 aliases and concurrent mutable state; Go race tests do not prove all races absent.
G3 sound bounded call graph and dynamic dispatch.
G4 full protected client-visible effects.
G5 completeness of the chosen client observation class.
G6 independently documented third-party modification authority vs app-only rights.
