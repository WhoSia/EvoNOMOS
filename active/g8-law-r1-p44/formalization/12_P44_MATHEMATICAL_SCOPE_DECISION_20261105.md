# P44 mathematical scope decision — 2026-11-05

## Research director judgment

Do not replace P44's central project of source-certified authorized, old-client-safe repair with generic online algorithms or reactive synthesis. Information timing is an important *index of the future-demand contract*, not a novel law or an excuse to start P45.

Central question: can a small, source-checkable, permission-respecting certificate soundly predict safe repair existence, obstruction or cost within a complete declared edit/observer grammar, and improve precision, certificate size, computation or transfer over strong classical source-aware rivals?

T4 PUBLIC ABSTRACTS: Pnueli/Rosner reactive synthesis; Borodin/El-Yaniv online computation; original source and exact theorem proof not yet read in session.
T5 OPEN SCIENTIFIC GATE: G1–G7 full source soundness, independent heldout maintainer tasks, actual effort and evidence of novelty.

### Next research sequence

A. Freeze finite stagewise trace/permission/cost semantics and the earlier released-demand invariant.
B. Establish a source-supported map from every concrete authorized safe edit path to cost-conservative abstract candidate paths; prove lower bound, and separately check one concrete witness for upper bound.

Publication remains a possible bounded formal-methods methods paper, NOT a proven universal software structure law. Continue within P44, PRs draft and LAW-R2 HOLD.
