## 2. Precise primary-source technical result
- §3.1 (printed p.605) defines a **rely-condition** `R_i(s,s')` on changes generated by the external environment, and a **guarantee-condition** `G_i(s,s')` bounding changes the component may cause. In the 1983 formulation both are relations on state pairs, with reflexive/transitive obligations in the author's stated context.
- §3.2 (printed pp.605–606), formula (3.7), states the central coexistence criterion for two parallel components: each guarantee must be at least as strong as the other process's rely, i.e., `G_A ⊆ R_B` and `G_B ⊆ R_A` when represented as binary relations over a common global state. There are separate inherited rely assumptions and whole-operation guarantee premises.
- Equations (3.9)–(3.13) require a **dynamic invariant** that survives task guarantees and relevant environment steps, plus conditions linking component postconditions to the overall postcondition. The two inclusions alone do not guarantee the desired whole-system postcondition or termination.
- §3.4 (printed pp.614–615) explicitly partitions the change spheres of two operations on a forest representation (root and body effects). This is very close to the modern narrative of an SRP-like effect partition. **Priority warning**: inferring 'independent effects permit modular proof' is not an EvoNOMOS mathematical discovery.

## 3. Mathematical relation to EvoNOMOS
Our P42 RC1 cost-labelled repair bisimulation and F27 product factorization are already classical in nature; F28–F31 build restricted source witnesses. Jones's executable-process changes are not automatically the same as **version transitions made by human authorized maintainers**: source grammar, actor rights, source versions, old-client observations, economic cost and future demand disclosure must be explicitly defined.

If one redefines an `edit guarantee` `G_i^{edit}` and an `edit rely` `R_j^{edit}`, then a compatibility obligation `G_i^{edit} ⊆ R_j^{edit}` is a direct classical analogy to Jones, **NOT a new theorem**. To lift it soundly from a real source repository, need source-version and build closure evidence, alias/effect proof, authenticated maintainer permissions, client-context preservation, cost and staged-demand witness; a relation merely over runtime states omits these dimensions. F31 currently only checks a frozen *three-file research module* and Go1.23.2 compilation (plus F30 fresh-local-pointer AST); it does not establish full repository provenance or rights.

## 4. Novelty and publication gate
Jones (1983) directly defeats any claim that 'we invented formal modular interference contracts from shared-state transitions'. O'Hearn, Yang & Reynolds (2009), Brookes (2004), and earlier separation logic intensify this priority barrier. A credible publishable result must demonstrate a **sound source-/rights-/history-bound extraction, prospective repair predictions or measured cost/precision advantage vs equally informed classical tools**, not rename rely–guarantee as SOLID superstructure.
