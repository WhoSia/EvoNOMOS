## Primary original papers in Drive

**Hammer, Phang, Hicks & Foster (2014), ADAPTON: Composable, Demand-Driven Incremental Computation.** [Drive original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KpoRmLWo9BVqVELu9BM2T577jCkTYzfM/view), PLDI 2014, DOI 10.1145/2594291.2594324. Abstract and introduction explicitly motivate reusing computation after small input changes, only reexecuting data dependencies demanded by observers; demanded computation graph and explicit inner computation/outer observer distinction. What it DOES NOT claim: source-certified organizational patch rights or an EvoNOMOS notion of structural-law validity. **P44 consequence:** comparing only full from-scratch source reanalysis is too weak; include a classical cached dependency analysis where relevant.

**Hammer et al. (2015), Incremental Computation with Names (Extended Version).** [Drive original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RIM6JI5mbrBrHQvwzGmfvYUkBRDrUNMH/view), OOPSLA 2015, DOI 10.1145/2814270.2814305. Abstract and semantics discuss first-class stable names and formal **from-scratch consistency** as correctness criterion for incrementally reused results; can outperform ordinary self-adjusting approaches on certain tasks. **P44 consequence:** if our certificate is reused on revision r+1, demand agreement with an independently computed from-scratch result, not merely a passing cached checksum.

**Diemert, Goodenough, Joyce & Weinstock (2023), Incremental Assurance Through Eliminative Argumentation.** [Drive original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10lixYjjdtlh47Bjx7CzyaYDZqlDNjQgv/view), Journal of System Safety 58(1), 7–15. The original article's abstract/intro distinguish a safety assurance case at one time from its subsequent evidence, which can strengthen OR WEAKEN its argument. Its proposed eliminative argumentation pattern articulates what doubts remain. **P44 consequence:** an immutable receipt hash protects certificate integrity only conditionally on an externally trusted anchor; it does not establish the model's continued applicability after source changes.

**Calcagno, Distefano, O'Hearn & Yang (2009), Compositional Shape Analysis by Means of Bi-Abduction.** [Drive original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pO5rUHggdq1FFBzU_bOkFokK7E2WvNuA/view), POPL 2009. Original abstract/intro discuss analyzing procedures independently from callers, inferring anti-frames (missing heap preconditions) and frames (unaffected resources). **P44 consequence:** the best classical competitor for future source repair is not only a string/AST cache but a compositional heap-footprint analysis with inferred preconditions. F18 toy's exact lexical cache does NOT beat bi-abduction.
