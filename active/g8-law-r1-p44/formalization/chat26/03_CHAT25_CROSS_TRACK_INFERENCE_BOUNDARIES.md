# Chat 26 — Cross-track Research Boundaries (Paper B / P44 / Mathematical Abstractor)

## Distinct questions

**Paper B:** Given an already fixed patch, a declared requirement and an applicable old-contract scope, when does a verdict about OCP justify accepting or rejecting that exact patch? Missing evidence may require HOLD. The actual behavior oracle and the normatively authored decision threshold must not be conflated.

**P44:** Given two initially matched source designs, what effects do *independently specified future* requirements and authorized edit choices produce on correctness, effort and verification across actual actors? A finite test pass does not identify a causal architecture ranking.

**Mathematics:** What information can be forgotten about a program without losing declared future observations and edit feasibility? Observation equivalence alone may not commute with source edits; a sufficient quotient must be stable under the declared action grammar.

## Shared schema, without collapsing the sciences

`world = (source, contract, history, rights, information, actor, demand, costs)`.

- Paper B holds patch and selected execution fixed, varies **known legitimate requirement/contract interpretations**, producing a robust ACCEPT/REJECT/HOLD judgement.
- P44 fixes independent pre-task design arms and seeks **observed potential repair outcomes**, changing source under a common demand/rights/budget protocol.
- Mathematical abstraction considers a quotient of state space and asks whether **observation and action transformers factor** through that quotient.

One can use version spaces in Paper B and repair right-languages in P44, but neither proves field adoption, engineer efficiency or a novel SOLID law. The proposed common object is a *bookkeeping superstructure*, not a demonstrated functor, fibration or scientific discovery.

## Direct falsifiers and negative controls

- P35: unmatched before-task output 415/204 disqualifies attribution under any contract including absent Content-Type.
- Paper B: if the proposed existing book-prefix contract is unknown, technical-report-only demand leaves opposite patch decisions. A model that issues unconditional acceptance from “OCP compliance” is unsound under the declared two-by-two world set.
- F37: if a later requirement is selected after inspecting a candidate's edited cost, tag NONBLIND even if the issue author is external.
- Abstractor: if q(s)=q(t) but action a is enabled at only one state, q does not suffice for future reachability; if q(a(s))≠q(a(t)) it cannot admit a deterministic no-reread update.
