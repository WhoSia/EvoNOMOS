## Scientific target and falsifier

The target is NOT to show that independent callbacks or shared handlers are inherently better. It is to test whether **the repair possibility and multiobjective repair cost of two initially behavior-matched source organizations reverses across independently specified future maintenance demands**, when source rights, client observations and information provided to competing predictors are controlled. A negative finding (equal feasible repair sets, a classical rival with identical predictions, or missing independent demands) is informative and must remain visible.

The estimand is demand-stratified: for structure z in {Independent, Shared} and externally authored task d, let R(z,d | C, A, B) be the set of permitted, prefix-old-client-safe source-edit traces respecting frozen contract C, authentic rights A, and budget B. Define a cost vector for each completed trace and use its nondominated frontier rather than scalarizing labor, changed-source sites, risk or test failures without justification. If actual human repair labor is not measured, use `SYNTHETIC_EDIT_COUNT` and do NOT call it engineering time. A difference in the observed implementation cost is not causal unless intervention assignment and other confounders are handled.

**Null / hard negative controls:** (i) both arms tie for all eligible tasks; (ii) a conventional source-aware planner reproduces every claimed prediction; (iii) effects previously invisible to the contract (interface method sets, typed-nil, aliases, HTTP wire detail) break initial matching, invalidating the pair; (iv) task-specific operator rights are unequal; (v) any claim uses an issue chosen after seeing that it favors a structure.

## Minimal information non-identification observation

If two possible maintenance environments w1,w2 produce identical information available to a planner at decision time but demand different uniquely safe repairs, no deterministic planner observing that information can be correct in both. This follows from an indistinguishability argument, NOT from a novel EvoNOMOS theorem. The right research move is to identify the missing observable or decline to predict; the wrong move is to count retrospective revelation as prospective success.
