# Rights symmetry and normalization debt

Both routers are granted the same abstract permission: add a new application-owned route handler and route registration. Both have the same three chronological future demands. Neither can patch upstream router internals. The initial adapters themselves are application-owned but were only required for Chi under the *specific* selected twelve-observer normalization grammar.

Synthetic edit units:
- baseline normalized Stdlib 0; Chi H+D+M 3;
- future demands add one registration+handler unit per route on both architectures;
- future incremental cost at stages 0,1,2,3 is 0,1,2,3 for both;
- historical plus prospective total work: Stdlib 0,1,2,3; Chi 3,4,5,6.

These counts depend on our explicitly bounded intervention grammar, not on actual labor or any universal design superiority. In a production codebase, normalization and later modifications could share code, so this calculation is a defined additive accounting scheme rather than a general equality theorem for maintenance time.
