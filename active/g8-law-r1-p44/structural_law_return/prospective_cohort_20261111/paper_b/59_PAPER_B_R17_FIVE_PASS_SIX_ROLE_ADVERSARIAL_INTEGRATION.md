## Pass 1 — Restore the research question (Founding thesis keeper, historical scholar)
**Question:** Is Paper B becoming an OCP problem-report paper again?
- v01–v21 mainly studied how OCP labels fail to settle particular patch dispositions; valuable but narrower than the **2026-07-29 idea** restored in v22.

**Decision:** lead with what AI agents do with conditional advice, then concretize using one OCP witness (v01 + v19). Historical background is a check on premises, not a causal history of AI dogmatism.

## Pass 2 — Formal and source-audit reviewer (requirements, contracts, language semantics)
**Question:** Is "operational quasi-axiomatization" analytically distinguishable from ordinary requirements checking?
- A static structural label is **not** sufficient for patch disposition in general; this is a classical result of missing program requirements, not a new impossibility theorem.
- v09/12 and v17's 3×3 table separates conditional Accept, Reject, and Defer when contracts withheld. This is persuasive as an authored witness, but finite-world verdicts depend on the stipulated world. The third independent obligation in v19 destroys overly broad approval inferences.
- v16 confirms a benchmark audit with **48 distinct submitted codes / 768 stored rows / 24 masked prompt scaffolds** and warns that 4 of 12 unique Python codes include comments: do not claim all context absent.
- v20's author-corrected Chi issue #1035 demonstrates the importance of source provenance. It is NOT an OCP-induced agent error.
**Decision:** restore a small, exact fixed-patch 2×2 contrast, cite full uncertainty grid and provenance in appendix; call principle→patch transport a **site of inquiry**, not new verifier.

## Pass 3 — Causal and experimental reviewer (identifiability and negative controls)
**Question:** Can a four-arm prompt study establish quasi-axiomatization?
- No: `HARD_SOLID` can legitimately cause compliance with a user instruction, and even a code change does not identify model beliefs/pretraining exposure. Source-backed contract evidence, explicit instruction precedence, actual right to abstain, and post-evidence patch disposition are required to characterize *candidate* unjustified authority.
- Do not conflating source task stratum with experimental arm: ALIGN and CONFLICT tasks must both exist, and the ordinary requirements-aware/classical rival gets the same task and authority facts.
- Outcome **lexical only** is negative evidence of behavior; **strong SOLID better** is a counterexample to an unconditional harm story; **no difference** a null; **unresolved authority hierarchy** unidentifiable.
**Decision:** research proposal states randomized arm comparisons identify **effects of injected advice**, not learned dogmatism; refuse to over-operationalize a latent mental state.
