# Paper B R15 — Adversarial Referee Audit of v26 and an Invitation That Could Fail
2026-11-22 · cross-language editorial research audit, **no independent human peer review performed**

## Verdict in one sentence
The re-centered paper now has a recognizable question, but a skeptical reviewer may still regard 'operational quasi-axiomatization' as rhetorical relabeling of instruction compliance, conventional requirements traceability, or automation bias. The paper must make its incremental empirical target, not its terminological novelty, the reason to read it.

## The strongest plausible rejections

### Objection A — Name without identifiable object
"'Quasi-axiomatization' is a memorable metaphor, not a scientific construct. You are measuring whether an agent breaks a contract."
Response boundary: The proposed scope is **jointly** (i) an explicitly invoked *named design principle*, (ii) its decision impact on patch disposition, (iii) authenticated case-relevant contrary evidence and authorized priority, and (iv) behavioral revision/abstention. This *operationalization is a hypothesis for a program of studies*, not an already validated construct. Do not claim it is a new psychological state. Existing literature on requirements traceability and instructions may already explain much or all of it; specify that negative result openly.

### Objection B — Built-in invalid treatment
"If you tell an agent HARD_SOLID, obeying is exactly correct. The experiment manufactures your alleged phenomenon."
Response boundary: the four-arm study can test causal effects of supplied guidance, NOT belief/dogmatism. Record legitimate adherence and explicit authority hierarchy. For 'quasi-axiomatization' more persuasive evidence, vary supplied authentic contract evidence only when authorized while preserving the task and hierarchy. A conflict with a higher-priority instruction is **not** evidence of unjustified insistence.

### Objection C — OCP is not five principles, and patterns are not SOLID
"'Operational quasi-axiomatization' of SOLID is generalized from OCP alone."
Response boundary: OCP is a *worked example*, not an identified effect across SRP/LSP/ISP/DIP. A falsifiable research agenda invites principle-by-principle replication, not a five-principle uniformity claim. Avoid treating GoF design patterns as synonyms of SOLID; they have a distinct history and earlier primary book.

### Objection D — Your research question may already exist
"Good engineers have always treated design rules contextually; why this paper?"
Response boundary: Prior art legitimately covers scoped advice, design rationale, contracts and coding-agent rules. B calls for a specific empirical audit of **the transfer of named principles from explanation into binding agent code decisions**, with explicit competing explanations and observable revisions. Even this wording may overlap others; no worldwide priority claim before fuller literature review.

### Objection E — "Researchers, please join" without entry points
Research agenda must give **minimal reproducible tasks** with a one-sentence falsifier for each.
A. Historian: sample manuals, docs and repository rules with dated metadata; code contextual qualifiers; falsifier: no evidence that stronger language strips scope.
B. Coding-agent evaluator: freeze one independent Go task, a valid alternate, protected old-client oracle, and four arms; falsifier: all agents choose equivalent correct repairs.
C. Requirements/PL researcher: provide documented counterexample and reasoned source-backed precedence; falsifier: decisions correct or appropriately abstain after rebuttal.
D. Empirical SE economist: measure initial/incremental token/time/review/race errors per task under matched workload; falsifier: costs are same or better without principle prompt.
Do not silently rebrand legacy original Go F37 wrapper ties as agent-arm data.
