# Paper B — Independent Review Validation Protocol (Proposed, Not Executed)

**Status:** no registered human cohort, no external reviewer responses and no F38 result. This is a prospective research design, not data in manuscript 17.

## 1. Competing conditions

For the same independently authored proposed patch and authentic previously documented requirement, provide identical code, source context and time budget:
1. Principle-label-only judgment with no verified source-to-contract map.
2. Contract-grounded judgment with explicit source-and-claim provenance and missing-premise questions.
3. Matched conventional requirements/compatibility checklist with exactly the same evidence and budget.

Differences between the second and third are central. A benefit that conventional review achieves equally is a classical TIE, not new method superiority.

## 2. Sampling and rights

Freeze the repositories, issue dates, language, issue-selection rules and all exclusions before viewing any candidate's cost or success. Log failures and noneligible samples. An existing external issue chosen after seeing the candidate programs is nonblind exploratory evidence. Preserve project-level clusters; repeated runs of one source file are not new independent cases. Do not infer upstream edit authority from ownership of a research fork.

## 3. Grounded evidence hierarchy

Record each claimed requirement's provenance, actor/source, URL, original timestamp, exact source SHA, affected old-client call route and whether maintainer adoption is explicit. Keep distinct: authoritative API contract and accepted requirement; observational tests and usage; provisional issue comments; implementation clues; design principle labels. Tests do not by themselves grant authority to protect every observable detail, and a label cannot independently establish a contract.

## 4. Independent adjudication

At least two reviewers who have not seen the intended judgment key should inspect candidate patches and source contracts. Allowed outcomes: ACCEPT, REJECT, CONDITIONAL, ABSTAIN, INCONSISTENT_CONTRACT. Keep disagreements and missing source evidence; no forced majority answer where the historical source remains ambiguous.

## 5. Outcomes and costs

Prespecify false rejections, false acceptances, unjustified confidence, evidence-source citations, abstention/contradiction rate, question count, actual review time, verified new/old call-path preservation, regression tests and source inspection cost. Use paired task comparisons and account for same-project dependence. Do not turn source line count into measured human effort or count identical repeated LLM submissions as independent cases. A binary paired significance test applies only to cases with independently established binary labels and genuinely matched observations.

## 6. Negative controls and abort gates

- Missing old API promise: withhold a binary verdict until source-backed protection is identified.
- Unsupported citation or incompatible contract facts: reject the inference and report provenance conflict.
- A correct patch labeled stylistically noncompliant: a principle label alone cannot justify rejection unless an actual obligation fails.
- A change satisfying the new request while breaking a protected old caller: require the old contract source to justify rejection.
- Conventional matched reviewer gives the same result: CLASSICAL TIE.
- Independently verified requirement cannot be found: mark NOT IDENTIFIED rather than fabricate a protected contract.

## 7. Paper B integration

The existing version-17 paper remains a conceptual, archival and researcher-authored executable example. A subsequent independent cohort would be a new empirical extension requiring its own ethics, documentation, sample registration, raw evidence custody and separate version. Until then, all industry prevalence and causal-improvement claims remain HOLD.
