# Paper B — Getting the Two 1968 SIMULA Sources

## S-10 — the Garmisch manuscript

1. Open the [National Library of Norway item](https://www.nb.no/items/7d94a1dc051ddb5538c01e344f878f0c). If the viewer does not load, open the [catalogue metadata](https://api.nb.no/catalog/v1/items/7d94a1dc051ddb5538c01e344f878f0c). The national bibliography record is `norbok/6801101nno`. It describes six paginated leaves in quarto, a stencil, and the NCC series number `[S 10]`, which should retain its brackets when quoted from the catalogue. This is a metadata record with `isDigital: false`, `pageCount: 0`, and zero page canvases in the returned IIIF manifest. No downloadable full text was found.
2. Send a request to **veiledningen@nb.no**. Ask the [reading-room staff](https://www.nb.no/en/reading-rooms/) to confirm the physical holding and quote a PDF reproduction of **all six leaves**, preserving the first leaf, all printed sides, and visible imprint or series marks. Ask about fees, delivery, and use conditions. The public [reading-room regulations](https://www.nb.no/content/uploads/2019/05/Reglement-for-Spesiallesesalen-2019.pdf) describe staff-mediated copy requests. The request has not been sent.
3. If NB cannot supply a copy, use the [National Archives general enquiry form](https://www.nasjonalarkivet.no/form-general-enquiry/). Cite Kristen Nygaard's private papers, **Z-8**, cross-reference **RA-C**, and [Knut Hegna's 11 September 2020 inventory, printed p. 45](https://www.ub.uio.no/fag/informatikk-matematikk/informatikk/faglig/bibliografier/dns/nygaard/KNiRiksarkivet.pdf#page=45). The inventory describes an undated stencil and gives no narrower RA-C box number for this item. Ask staff to confirm the current collection and box reference and whether they can supply a scan. The [National Archives service declaration](https://www.nasjonalarkivet.no/service-declaration/) explains the exact-reference requirement.
4. For a parallel holdings check, forward the exact record number and series number to the [UiO Informatics Library](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/about/contact-us/) at **informatikkbiblioteket@ub.uio.no**, then to [Norsk Regnesentral](https://nr.no/en/about/) at **nr@nr.no** for current custody or an archive referral. The [Garmisch report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XE3E8MGUtSVtKvgdBe5V4qxaFZ-4YDLr/view), printed p. 157, lists the title without a page locator for a full reprint. Nygaard and Dahl's [historical bibliography](https://www.cs.tufts.edu/comp/150FP/archive/kristen-nygaard/hopl-simula.pdf), p. 479, identifies P. M. Kjeldaas as the presenter. The report also lists a different working paper under Kjeldaas's name.

**Subject** — Digital copy request for NCC S-10 (1968), NORBOK 6801101nno

Dear National Library staff,

I am researching the history of early software design. Could you confirm whether you hold *Some uses of the external class concept in SIMULA 67* (Ole-Johan Dahl, Bjørn Myhrhaug and Kristen Nygaard, 1968)? Your national bibliography records it as **norbok/6801101nno**, Norsk Regnesentral publication **[S 10]**, six paginated leaves.

If permitted, could I order a PDF scan of all six leaves, including the first leaf and any series or publication markings, for research use in South Korea? Please let me know the cost, delivery options and use conditions before proceeding. If a copy cannot be supplied, a current shelfmark or referral to another holding library would help.

Thank you for your help.

## S-2 — the Common Base Language first printing

1. Open the [permanent link for the digital scan](https://urn.nb.no/URN:NBN:no-nb_digibok_2017102708110). NB's [digital metadata](https://api.nb.no/catalog/v1/items/d65f11d406f2d4dde908bc1426b0fa09) and [NORBOK record 6801100nno](https://api.nb.no/catalog/v1/items/8dab436d64ddc9b1a4a5f58a1bacde3c) identify the 1968 publication, 141 pages plus four leaves. The digitization has 157 image entries and is tagged `Tilgang for norske IP-adresser`.
2. For access from South Korea, open [Digital access from abroad](https://www.nb.no/en/access-to-bokhylla-from-abroad/), download **Application form digital access from abroad**, complete and sign it, and send it to **bokhyllatilgang@nb.no** with the URN and short research purpose. If approved, the library describes six months of reading access. For a downloadable file, separately ask NB whether a copy is available under its reproduction terms.
3. For physical holdings or document supply, give the [UiO Informatics Library](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/about/contact-us/) legacy BIBSYS **961238992** and [Oria identifier 999612389924702202](https://www.oria.no/?vid=NB&search=999612389924702202), plus May 1968 and NCC Publication S-2. Ask for a current shelfmark or reproduction estimate. The [Oria instructions](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/using/search-the-library/oria-help/ordering-in-oria.html) describe local-library requests for readers abroad. Verify the first printing using the supplied title and imprint. Another catalogue entry reuses S-2 for a revised printing of the same extent, and the open CHM scan is S-22 (October 1970).
