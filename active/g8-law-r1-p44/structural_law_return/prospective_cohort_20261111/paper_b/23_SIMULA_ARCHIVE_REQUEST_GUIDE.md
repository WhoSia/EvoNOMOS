# Paper B — Requesting the Two 1968 SIMULA Documents

## What to ask for

| Document | Locator | Request and edition check |
|---|---|---|
| *SIMULA 67 Common Base Language* | Norwegian Computing Center Publication S-2 (1968); 141 numbered pages plus four leaves in [UiO Library's 2016 bibliography](https://www.ub.uio.no/fag/naturvitenskap-teknologi/informatikk/faglig/bibliografier/simula.pdf), item [27]. Its INF code indicates a physical holding recorded by the Informatics Library. | Ask for the current call number or Oria permalink and an estimate for a full scan. If a full scan is unavailable, ask for front matter and the complete declaration or use sections on external classes if present. Confirm S-2 and 1968 from the document itself. The S-22 revision is dated 1970. |
| *Some Uses of the External Class Concept in SIMULA 67* | [Ole-Johan Dahl bibliography](https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/about/ole-johan-dahl/bibliography/), item 33, NR Doc. 1968. Context: NATO Software Engineering Conference, Garmisch, October 1968. [Garmisch conference report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XE3E8MGUtSVtKvgdBe5V4qxaFZ-4YDLr/view), printed p. 157. | Ask for the stand-alone conference handout, its archival ID or repository, and a complete scan if allowed. If the library cannot locate it, ask whether the records are held by UiO special collections or the original publisher. No current shelfmark or open full text has been verified. |

1. Open the [University of Oslo Library contact page](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/about/contact-us/) and find **Informatics Library in Ole-Johan Dahls hus**. Send the request to **informatikkbiblioteket@ub.uio.no**. Use the two bibliography identifiers as written above and ask for reproduction cost and conditions before ordering.
2. If they point to Oria, search **SIMULA 67 Common Base Language** and confirm the publication series, date, and extent in the record. Follow the [Oria request instructions](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/using/search-the-library/oria-help/ordering-in-oria.html). If the catalogue requires a UiO account, a local school or university librarian can inquire about document supply or interlibrary loan.
3. If the handout is outside the regular catalogue, ask [UiO Digitisation and Special Collections](https://www.ub.uio.no/english/using/digitisation/) and, if needed, [Norwegian Computing Center](https://nr.no/en/about/) at **nr@nr.no** for an archive referral. Ask them to distinguish a retained copy from a bibliography reference.
4. Save any supplied scan with the reply date, call number, any usage conditions, and a note identifying the cover, imprint, and page range. These documents are a technical report and a stand-alone conference handout as described by UiO.

## Draft inquiry

**Subject** — Digitisation inquiry for two 1968 SIMULA 67 documents

Dear Informatics Library team,

I am researching the history of early software design and looking for two SIMULA 67 documents.

1. *SIMULA 67 Common Base Language*, Norwegian Computing Center Publication S-2 (1968), 141 numbered pages plus four leaves. It appears as item [27] in the University of Oslo Library's SIMULA bibliography. I am looking for this 1968 edition.
2. *Some Uses of the External Class Concept in SIMULA 67*, O.-J. Dahl, B. Myhrhaug and K. Nygaard, NR Doc. (1968), presented at the NATO Software Engineering Conference in Garmisch in October 1968. It appears as item 33 in the Ole-Johan Dahl bibliography.

Could you confirm the present holdings and provide the call numbers or catalogue links? If scans can be supplied for research, please let me know the cost and conditions. If a complete scan of S-2 is unavailable, the title and publication pages, contents, and any section dealing with external classes would help. If the conference handout is held elsewhere, I would appreciate a referral.

Thank you for your help.
