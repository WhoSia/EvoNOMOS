## The precise bridge v18 now makes

A classification of source structure can be true across the authored alternate contracts, while acceptability of an identical patch changes with which obligations legitimately bind. One cannot promote classification correctness to a unique patch rejection without adequate demand, protected old-client obligations and call-path authority. Even though each fact is conventional, the bounded, versioned demonstration is useful as a methodological critique of *unjustified transfer of assessment uses*, consistent with Kane (2013) and classical requirements traceability.

This is an **internal deductive example**, not a demonstration of causal over-axiomatization in actual workplaces. The paper's ambition should next shift from more authored toy cases to independent repository-level source-authority identification and matched conventional reviewer comparisons.
