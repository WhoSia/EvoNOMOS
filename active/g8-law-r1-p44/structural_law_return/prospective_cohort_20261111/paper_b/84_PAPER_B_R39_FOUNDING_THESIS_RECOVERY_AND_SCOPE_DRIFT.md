In the same first archive, EvoNOMOS 0.4 describes a loop: **principle hypothesis generation → structural intervention → dynamic change experiment → causal effect estimate → multiobjective evolution → distillation to human-readable principles**. Its reports concerned synthetic or limited source-specific tests, NOT a validated new general law. This is the ambitious research direction v33 buried in §4 as a loose aside.

The writing sequence shifted from original architectural epistemology into a narrow "name vs verdict" formulation during repeated novelty defense. The narrowing was expedient because label/role effects could be connected to already understood instruction work, but it changed the research objective. **Scientific caution must control assertions, not choose the easiest problem.** One can propose a hard scientific question without claiming to have solved it.

## Core research object (recover)
Whether historically important SOLID principles, treated not as mandatory axioms but as **defeasible, conditional structural hypotheses**, can be evaluated against real change/contract/maintainer environments, and whether a general framework can explain their success/failure or produce better conditional principles. AI agents matter in two roles: (1) agents risk importing the mnemonic as a universal command; (2) agents are also possible implementers/evaluators and participants in the principle-discovery loop.

### What the short position paper may legitimately claim
- Object-oriented design contains distinctive and **heterogeneous** ideas; Martin 1996's *strategic closure* is strong original textual evidence of conditional reasoning.
- Different maintenance regimes, protected behavior, time horizon and evaluators can change which design intervention is preferable. A generalized decision problem can be formulated as a **research hypothesis** without establishing empirical universality.
- Labels like “OCP violation” and “must” risk masking the missing empirical and contractual premises. This is a **symptom**, not the core scientific endpoint.
- An open research program could systematically **construct and disprove** conditional principles; include principle-induction, not just auditing SOLID.
- "All SOLID reduce to one higher equation" is unknown and could be false, especially as LSP is a semantic substitutability obligation that is not one more maintenance-cost heuristic.

### What cannot be claimed
- Historical prevalence of SOLID quasi-axiomatization in model training, or any specific model's internal beliefs.
- Measured reduction of cost, token usage, maintenance harm or universal superiority of a newer principle.
- Already discovered unifying mathematical principle, novel theorem, or experiments not actually executed.
- Novelty of generic instruction conflict tests or repository precedent effects; two original 2026 uploaded PDFs already establish those.
- Semantic correctness as merely a soft term in a cost utility; a protected contract can be a feasibility constraint.

## Nontrivial scientific obstacle not to evade
Given source code and a new requirement, one does not know the actual future change distribution, stakeholder preference weights, legitimate protected client observations, or maintainer capabilities. Without such information **no broad context-free ranking** of architecture variants is usually warranted. Even if that information is stated, the research may only identify a Pareto set or incomparable policies. The central scientific challenge is not merely to explain why OCP works *afterwards*, but to discover **prospectively usable features** for predicting which intervention helps under future changes and when to abstain. That is a meaningfully harder objective than auditing whether a sentence sounds categorical.

Also, LSP (semantic behavioral subtyping) is not just an average maintenance advantage: an attempted reduction of all five to cost needs to preserve semantic feasibility separately. An unifying structure may only be a family of different constraints and objectives. One precise failure of a reduction is productive.

## Paper B v34 recommended narrative
1. Open with user's originating paradox: how did conditional advice become something an agent/reviewer can treat as a veto, if the right design depends on possible changes and protected behavior?
2. Quickly show a source-grounded Martin example and a domain shift that reverses the preferred architectural intervention.
3. State the central unknown: could we identify the **conditions and hidden structure** in which principles are useful and discover new, better-scoped ones instead of obeying acronyms?
4. Introduce a bounded program: formulate conditional candidates, compare interventions while preserving contracts, observe lifecycle outcomes, refute/revise/abstain, induct candidate relations.
5. Close on epistemic autonomy. The next law is not decreed; it earns authority from tested scope. Mention lexicon and agent-specific behavior as one lens, not full thesis. Do not pretend empirical work is done.
