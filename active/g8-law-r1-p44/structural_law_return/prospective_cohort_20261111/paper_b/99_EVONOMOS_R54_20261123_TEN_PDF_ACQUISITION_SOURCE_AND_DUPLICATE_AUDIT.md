## Mathematical and epistemological insights — separate from source facts

**A. Baldwin 2023 supplies a precedent for *theory self-criticism*, not an indictment of SOLID.** Its author gives precise accounts of why predictions and real-options valuations are difficult without credible event frequencies. It is a compelling comparison for a thesis against *treating contingent advice as incontestable*.

**B. 'Design rule' has multiple disciplinary meanings.** LaMantia et al.'s constraints between stable interface choices and hidden modules are not the same entity as a normative “you violated OCP; must refactor” sentence. Suh's deliberately declared engineering axioms represent a third sense. Distinguish **semantic/architectural dependency condition**, **heuristic principle**, **organization-wide authorized policy**, and **formal postulate** before claiming things were “axiomatized.”

**C. Existing science supports the broader vision.** Räihä 2010 and Fu 2012 show rich architectures-as-search/architecture-as-change-propagation mathematics; that does not make the user's **novel reinterpretation and quasi-axiom critique** valueless. These are precedents to synthesize.

**D. Old evaluation functions can carry unnoticed authority.** A software-search algorithm may optimize cohesion/coupling because those objectives were chosen by designers, not because they are timeless laws. **Candidate new conceptual question:** Where did the objective/fitness function get its authority? What alternatives were ruled out by declaring it the score? This bridges game theory, value theory, optimization, software design principles and the user's original epistemic autonomy issue. This is our synthesis, not claimed as a direct statement by Räihä.

**E. Prediction remains a separate optional research track**, not a hurdle before submitting an idea paper. Zhan 2025, Germanos 2023 and Mishra 2026 are useful for a later empirical method but should not balloon the concise mathematical position paper.
