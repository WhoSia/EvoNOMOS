## Single proposition

> A software-design principle may be useful as a revisable heuristic, but its name alone does not warrant treating it as a decisive rule for accepting or rejecting a particular software change. With coding agents, this passage from advice to action can be studied as **operational quasi-axiomatization**, a behavioral, context-sensitive hypothesis that must be distinguished from ordinary instruction following, formal axiomatization, and the long-run sociological canonization of SOLID.

The manuscript is a **historical-critical position**. It argues that the phenomenon is a worthwhile, falsifiable target and proposes measurements; it does not assert how frequent it is or attribute it to opaque foundation-model pretraining.

## Mandatory argument spine
1. A concrete and non-straw-man opening: a legitimate code review asks whether a proposed change breaks the old caller contract, not just whether it exhibits a recognizable OCP pattern.
2. Historical rationale: Parnas, Meyer, Martin and Liskov/Wing supply different, condition-sensitive standards; Martin's strategic closure is explicitly relative to anticipated changes. Do NOT allege that Martin posited mathematical axioms.
3. The conceptual move: structural classification is distinct from disposition of a particular patch. Requirements, preserved client observations, permitted edits and alternative repairs matter. This insight has classical antecedents in contracts/requirements; novelty is the **specific behavioral research question in AI-mediated principle use**, not invention of contracts.
4. One bounded witness: the authored fixed-patch library reconstruction varies errata requirement and old book prefix protection. Report its exact author-defined scope; it proves neither naturalistic prevalence nor agent behavior.
5. An appropriately characterized benchmark: Pehlivan et al. 2025 study detection/classification, not patch approval; archived OCP subset counts 48 submitted examples, 768 rows, 24 masked templates under prior independent audit. Do not call them 768 independent experiments.
6. The most serious objection: an agent following HARD_SOLID may simply obey a legitimate current instruction. Behavior after binding contrary source evidence and policy precedence is an identification challenge, not a simple word-frequency metric.
7. An executable *future* proposal: four frozen instruction arms, independently pinned Go task, same source/contract/oracle, aligned and conflicting task strata, fresh contexts, code vs explanation, valid strict guidance and lexical-only rivals. No agent effect measured yet.
8. A decisive, humble conclusion: measure the authority actually exercised by principle language; software design can keep principles conditional, corrigible and empirically answerable.
