## Critical distinction discovered in this audit: compliance != epistemic dogmatism

An agent obeying `HARD_SOLID` may simply comply with explicit task instruction. It is not thereby evidence that the agent has treated a principle as an independently justified axiom; nor that pretraining encoded SOLID as axioms. Even violation of a protected client test can be caused by overconstrained or inconsistent instructions. This source-of-authority confound must appear in B's discussion and the follow-up experimental design; keep original P35-B1 four-arm protocol frozen. See separate R11 note.
