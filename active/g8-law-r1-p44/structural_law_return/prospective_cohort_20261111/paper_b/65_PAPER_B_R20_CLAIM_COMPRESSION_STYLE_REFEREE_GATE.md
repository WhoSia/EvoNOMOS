# Paper B R20 — Claim-Compression, Style and Scholarly Challenge Gate
Date 2026-11-23. Basis: directly read v01–27 plus R19A–D sentence-lineage indexes. No external peer review and no new agent experimental results.

## Under the wrapping, one research idea
**Raw nucleus:** A named software-design principle may be treated as having greater authority over a specific AI-mediated patch decision than the actual requirements and protected old clients justify. Is this empirically distinguishable from ordinary instruction following, source misunderstanding, and accepted requirements reasoning? Under what conditions?

If this were only "developers should use SOLID contextually", the paper would add little: its antecedents are classical. The **incremental question** is specifically whether and how AI coding/review agents *operationalize* named principles in final patch choices, and whether authenticated contradictory evidence changes those choices. This is a proposed object of research, not demonstrated prevalence or novelty established against every work.

## Independent internal critique axes with resolutions
| Lens | Attack | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Originality | "You rediscovered contracts." | Explicitly attribute requirements reasoning to prior art; novelty is a testable **agent decision-authority question**, conditional on more exhaustive related-work check. |
| Causal | "HARD_SOLID compliance is dogmatism?" | No; differentiate authorized higher-priority policy and agent belief. Randomization identifies injected-instruction effect only. |
| Historical | "Martin was dogmatic?" | No; show strategic closure and responsibility, never assert 1996 author stated universal axioms. |
| Narrative | "You have one sentence of idea and 5,000 words of costume." | Cut to ca. 7 short sections, motivating specific scene, one constructed case, one proposed test, one serious objection, an invitation. |
| Style | "An LLM wrote seven numbered sentences that all start 'We propose'." | Mix declarative and interrogative sentences; use concrete nouns and a sharp closing question, avoid breathless self-advertisement, avoid puns that reduce scientific credibility. |
| Controls | "The label for conditional authority is unmeasurable." | Define criterion by authenticated source, permitted action, reviewer independence, revision, and explicit unresolved. |
| Resources | "Fewer files means fewer tokens/energy." | Separate future longitudinal, never assert energy benefit from source edits. |
| Translation | "Korean rhetorical question might sound leading." | Check intent/negation with English at the same claim strength; do not literal translate Korean word order. |

## Editorial score contract
- 0.00–1.00 is the **editor's subjective recommendation to retain wording**, NOT a frequency, confidence interval, posterior probability, text quality fact or factual accuracy score.
- Each retained KO and EN sentence has an independent record: sentence ID, text, recommendation, argumentative role, necessity/risk/comment. Remove all weak sentences before calling the manuscript 'clean'; keep low-score **alternatives** separate and explicitly excluded.
- Strong wording can still need bibliographic verification. Flag verification as a separate requirement, not lower a confidence score to fake probability.
- Use green for ≥0.90, orange for 0.65–0.89, red for <0.65 only within the author/editor document. No unexplained color scoring.
- Record the sentence correspondence in the two languages but reject mechanical 1:1 translation where natural prose differs.
