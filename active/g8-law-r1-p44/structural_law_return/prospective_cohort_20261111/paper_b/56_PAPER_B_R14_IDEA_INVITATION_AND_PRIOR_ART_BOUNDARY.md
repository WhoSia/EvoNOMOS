# Paper B R14 — Idea Invitation vs Evidence Claim: a Research-Priority Reframe
2026-11-22 · research note · no new coding-agent observations

## Owner clarified the intended contribution
The project does not currently claim to discover a universal law, disprove SOLID, or measure a causal training-origin mechanism. Paper B is deliberately an **idea-led, falsifiable position** inviting outside researchers to investigate whether, when, and with which consequences named software-design principles gain unjustified authority in AI-assisted engineering.

This is not a claim of worldwide historical priority or exclusive ownership of a problem. Ideas cannot be validated by authorship of a ChatGPT conversation; public scholarship must locate the contribution relative to literature.

## Revised thesis and scope
**Research object:** the *operative authority* of design-principle language over patch disposition, architectural choice, and revision in the presence of requirement, contract, and counterevidence.

**Question, not answer:** Does a model or reviewing process promote a revisable recommendation into an overriding constraint? How does this interact with authorized instruction hierarchy, source evidence and client obligations?

**Positive scope:** influential named advice from SOLID and patterns as representative mechanisms; classification→decision transport; explicit instruction following as an alternative explanation; source-backed client-contract adjudication; null effects; legitimate strict-principle success; protocol for independent agents/reviewers.

**Excluded scope:** formal axioms are inherently bad; SOLID is wrong; Go is purely functional; a new F=ma-style theory has been found; OOP is inherently less efficient; token/energy savings are established; proprietary training corpora induced dogmatism; one paper can settle the whole question.

## Critical weakening of a previous informal argument
There is no accepted demonstration that the five SOLID principles are actually axioms, that a foundation model internally believes them, or that OOP predominance caused model-generated code defects. The word *quasi* is not a license for psychological mind-reading. The right unit of observation is an externally visible **disposition or generated patch after source-grounded counterevidence**.

A hard-SOLID instruction may be a binding user policy. Its obedience is not misapplication. The study must distinguish explicit policy compliance, incorrect patch for other reasons, refusal after source-backed rebuttal, and uncertain/abstaining behavior. In a position paper, these are *proposed research dimensions*, not operational measurements already achieved.

## Work that would actually recruit other researchers
- A one-paragraph problem statement readable without EvoNOMOS jargon.
- A single worked source/contract thought experiment; link full reconstructive evidence rather than occupying the main thesis.
- Four independent research invitations with sharply defined falsifiers: historical documentation, agent intervention, paradigm transfer, efficiency/energy/maintenance cost. Do not make the four into equally established results.
- An explicit 'What would make us change our minds?' paragraph with null result, doctrinal gain, and equal classical alternatives.
- Concise accessible Markdown draft (English and Korean), source ledger separate, specific research questions and open protocols.

## Publication correctness gate
A research agenda, however promising, does not itself confer academic priority; publication, preprints, independent citation and review are separate actions. Historical origin in July 2026 is internal provenance, not proof of first discovery. Exact independent experiment count remains ZERO. Future claims about tokens, energy or law-finding require full measurements and competing baselines.
