- **13–21:** More precise risk, source provenance, adverse classical precedents, uncertainty; depth and credibility improve but the center remains OCP/patch judgment.

Neither 01 nor 26 automatically supersedes the other as prose: **v27 should use v26's thesis + v09/17/19 concrete counterexample + v16 documentary precision + v22 original four-arm frontier**.

### B. Evidence strength
- Historical Meyer/Martin analysis: based on preserved primary-source inspections; later independent quotation/page verification still advisable.
- Benchmark counts: archive/source audit reconstructed earlier; never confuse 48 examples with 768 independent tasks; some code comments provide local hints.
- Library fixed patch: researcher-constructed examples demonstrate conditional disposition in a stipulated finite world, not prevalence of dogmatic reviewers.
- Chi issues: directly authored public demand and documented workaround, not observed doctrinal rejection.
- Four-arm agents and token/energy benefit: **proposed, ZERO observed randomized agent treatment effects**.
- LAW-R2, universal theory: HOLD. No candidate law is established by Paper B.

### C. What should not return
1. Inventing a second contribution called the 'principle-to-patch transport verifier' when contracts/requirements are classical.
2. Strong causal claim that public SOLID literature was necessarily learned as dogma by a particular opaque foundation model.
3. A universal criticism of all five SOLID principles inferred from one OCP witness.
4. A class-vs-functions or Go-vs-Java style contest as a second thesis; the Go case belongs in supplementary notes.
5. Estimating maintenance cost or tokens from count of edited files; early v12 explicitly disavows that leap.
6. Conflating (a) obeying authorized HARD_SOLID instruction with (b) unreasonably preferring a principle over a genuinely binding contract.
7. Mistaking `unquestioned` in the title for a measured outcome; prefer interrogative, non-prejudicial title in next manuscript.
