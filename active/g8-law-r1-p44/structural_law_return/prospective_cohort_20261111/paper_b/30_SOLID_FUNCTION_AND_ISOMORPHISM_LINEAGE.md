Archive reconstruction and functional research notes, 2026-11-17 KST. The Paper B manuscript uses the history of judgments about OCP. This parallel record carries the broader structural question.

## What the project was trying to preserve

The [Origin & Drift Audit](https://app.notion.com/p/3c8ef561cf9281faa057d75c35762837) records two connected aims. Test which design intervention pays under which maintenance demands, then ask whether a smaller set of mechanisms predicts both the benefits and their reversals. In the [August 29 DIP-27 note](https://app.notion.com/p/3cbef561cf9281bc9848cbf7c7bf25b6), an isomorphism concerns the semantic image of an admissible intervention and whether it can be reversed. Syntactic rollback can leave an observable history behind. The later SOLID question has a different object.

[October 8, 16:59:09 KST, Archive 13, lines 4625–4629](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Lq-YckFbGn-TSJzwgerrkTfALb-5hpHC/view) contains the owner's original proposal, including “동형으로 정사영화?” and “경험적으로 가다가 구조를 이론적으로 찾는 거거든.” [Archive 14](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TuweqOEc1KY14ddEJVoPbBJAiT-i3thP/view) distinguishes current observations from future change sequences. [Archive 15](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zQ9j6GmfFaijbfz024-CQ2sJpj_SnpJV/view) asks for a proof-and-counterexample style of research. The original wording is now available alongside the [P31 reconstruction](https://github.com/WhoSia/EvoNOMOS/blob/bec30c70177c4f11c1a43427aec8032ff897ddb1/active/g8-law-r1-p31/P31_DEMAND_TRACE_CONGRUENCE_OO_BRIDGE.md), which specifies a finite deterministic model. For fixed demand alphabet and observer, states are equivalent if every finite demand word leaves the same observed outcome. That equivalence is the coarsest congruence preserving those observations. The quotient's uniqueness up to isomorphism applies to that declared model.

## Plausible functional values and their tests

| Principle | What it may buy | What the comparison must preserve or vary |
|---|---|---|
| SRP | Fewer boundaries involved in changing one independently evolving responsibility. | Vary the rate at which duties change together. Record coordination and duplicate edits as well as the number of touched files. |
| OCP | A recurring kind of change can be implemented through a predictable boundary while protected clients continue to work. | Name the change family and the protected boundary. Count edits and rebuilds separately from client observations. Change the demand family and retest. |
| LSP | Existing clients can rely on the supertype's guarantees during substitution, including the histories they can observe for mutable objects. | Preserve the declared client contract under substitution. An additional subtype operation may be compatible with that contract. Test shared state and admissible call sequences. [Liskov & Wing 1994](https://doi.org/10.1145/197320.197383). |
| ISP | Clients take on fewer operation and conformance obligations irrelevant to their work. | Specify the operations each client needs. Measure which clients an unrelated provider change reaches, and check how much shared consistency work moves across separated interfaces. |
| DIP | High-level policy can remain stable as an implementation is exchanged. | Freeze the policy and swap implementations. Record initial indirection, later edit and rebuild propagation, and whether runtime ownership or aliasing still couples the two. |

These are distinct candidates for explanation and measurement. A future empirical comparison should preregister what a representation must preserve, then seek matched designs that it collapses and whose maintenance outcomes separate. Chi's lost registration order supplies a concrete pattern for a distinguishing case. A held-out requirement and independently specified client contract are still needed for predictive claims. Paper B inherits the question of what an OCP judgment has to preserve. Its historical narrative can be tested without a universal representation of the five principles.
