### Working name and compact definition

**EvoNOMOS Principle Atlas Hypothesis (PAH) — 설계 원칙 지도 가설**

> Named design heuristics may be local, overlapping descriptions of design conditions rather than independent universally binding axioms. Their apparent universal authority may partly reflect coordination and institutional transmission. We ask whether existing mathematics can express their domains, relationships and defeasible status, without assuming a single theorem subsumes every principle.

Three separately answerable questions:
1. **Authority**: Why does a conditional guideline behave like a de facto axiom in reviews/AI conventions? History/game-theoretic coordination (hypothesis).
2. **Structure**: What higher-level formal relationships, if any, organize named design principles across contexts? Local-chart metaphor, FCA/Galois scopes, compositional constraints (possibilities, not proofs).
3. **Revision**: When does contextual counterevidence defeat a principle application? Dung-style argumentation, AGM/Darwiche–Pearl belief revision (existing mathematics reused).

**Not required for an honest short position piece:** universal proof, a completed randomized OCP-agent test, a full historical claim about training corpora, an algebra encompassing all five SOLID principles, or a global uniqueness certificate.

### Comparative thought experiment: de-jure vs de-facto axiom

Case A: A team explicitly adopts the **Information Axiom** of one specified Axiomatic Design model as a criterion with formal interpretation and scope. The design rule has stated authority and mathematical claims requiring evaluation.

Case B: A coding reviewer writes “OCP violation, must fix” without citing a repo policy, protected behavior, expected change or authoritative review standard. This may be technically correct, but its **binding force is underexplained**.

Neither case proves actual misconduct or universal dogmatism. The difference is *what warrants treating a proposition as binding*: explicit theoretical postulate, authorized project policy, or revisable heuristic judgment.

## One distinctive explanatory contrast: equilibria are not axioms

For the hypothetical two-maintainer coordination game in [R52](97_EVONOMOS_R52_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES_HIGHER_MATH_IDEA_ATLAS.md), both players following convention S is a Nash equilibrium with hypothetical payoff (3,3), while another mutually coordinated alternative F gives (4,4); unilateral deviation is costly. A convention can remain a stable norm even when a different collective choice dominates in that invented payoff table. **Stability of a practice is logically weaker than universal correctness of its technical design content**. This bridge must be tested historically, not asserted as SOLID's actual origin.

## What would an Atlas actually require to become a theorem?

- A rigorously specified design-space object, not a rhetorical claim that software is a manifold.
- Defined regions where each named heuristic's recommendation is justified, and measurable criteria for overlap/conflict.
- Valid transition interpretations on overlapping domains. Classical smooth manifold transition charts demand specific homeomorphisms/differentiable structure; **we have not supplied any** and should not pretend to.
- Evidence structure allowing unknown/refuted/supported and multiple incommensurable concerns (LSP contract semantics vs OCP future cost).
- An informative phenomenon not already explained by Alexander's pattern languages, FCA context lattices, Suh's axioms, information hiding, or standard modularity valuation.

As an **idea** this can be put forward today without having proved all of the above. The next scientific stage would determine which mathematical representation, if any, best fits actual design evidence.
