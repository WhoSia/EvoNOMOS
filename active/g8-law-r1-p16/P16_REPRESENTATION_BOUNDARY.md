# P16 Representation Boundary — Operational Minimality versus Arbitrary Recoding

P16 distinguishes two different claims.

1. **Operational primitive minimality under the frozen EvoNOMOS coordinate grammar.** This is testable. A retained coordinate must be prospectively defined, action-blindly recoverable, transported across at least two domains, deletion-necessary, and non-derivable from the other retained coordinates under exact interventions.
2. **Universal coordinate-cardinality minimality under arbitrary representation changes.** This is not claimed. Any finite tuple can be injectively packed into one symbol or scalar label. Such packing changes representation but does not reduce primitive content.

The P16 constitution already required semantic quotients rather than label concatenation. P16 therefore treats a legitimate merge as a **non-injective semantic coarsening** that preserves the intended operational meaning of the merged coordinate. Bijective tuple packing, hashing, arbitrary indexing, or reversible recoding is representation equivalence, not primitive reduction.

This boundary is consistent with earlier P12 rulings:

- generic minimal-state, feature-selection, and state-abstraction novelty were already killed;
- universal (X_{min}) was not claimed;
- EvoNOMOS-specific authority is restricted to anti-rationalization, software-context operationalization, prospective intervention grammar, and cross-domain replication.

Drive literature already supplies the relevant representation/abstraction boundary:

- Schölkopf et al. (2021), *Towards Causal Representation Learning*;
- Geiger et al. (2025), *Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability*;
- Sutter et al. (2025), *The Non-Linear Representation Dilemma*;
- Li, Walsh & Littman (2006), *Towards a Unified Theory of State Abstraction for MDPs*.

Accordingly, a positive P16 Court may establish only:

> **a reproducible operational minimal primitive basis under the frozen EvoNOMOS context grammar and admissible semantic quotients.**

It may not establish:

- a unique latent decomposition of software context;
- a representation-invariant minimum number of coordinates under arbitrary bijections;
- a domain-complete ontology of software design;
- novelty for generic minimal-state or state-abstraction theory.

This is a scope boundary, not a post-hoc rescue. It narrows the meaning of the presealed word "quotient" without changing any P16 pass/fail threshold.
