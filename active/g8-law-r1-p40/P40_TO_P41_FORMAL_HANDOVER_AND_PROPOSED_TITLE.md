## 2. Exact reason to stop lengthening P40 Math-B

P40 Math-B's P1–P6 sequence investigated the source/behavior gap deeply enough for a next-stage reformulation:

1. Typed local edit acceptance alone fails to establish global composition. 
2. Old-client observations must include headers and hidden handler side effects when those are contractual.
3. Source admission and successful combined client obligations are distinct: Echo's source-accepted duplicate overwrite breaks one independent new-client promise.
4. chi's parent mount reservation, httprouter's method-indexed source radix tree and Echo's parameter/static routing trees have different concrete admission predicates; input-language disjointness is not sufficient for implementation-agnostic assembly.
5. 16 P4 bounded forecasts were frozen before native tests and matched original source on all 16. P5's issue-derived forecasts were separately frozen and matched 6/6 with two declared abstentions; these are controlled source-contract tests, not independent empirical quality estimates.
6. Original Echo #2619 shows a **source-admitted but wrong-handler** countertrace: the source's route identity contract can fail after successful registration.
7. The new P6 selectively extracts the actual Echo HEAD routing case from AST, generates executable Go code, and verifies all 8 Boolean source selections against untouched native Echo. A **unique one-bit repair certificate** applies only to the three allowed Boolean toggles and a fixed HEAD-no-GET-effect goal.
8. Lean proves restricted conditional source-selector, abstract-transition and bounded-repair properties; full formal Go semantics refinement is still missing.
9. Accurate **classical CSP, data-refinement, separation/frame and source-aware guarded semantics survived** every present bounded court. No new universal mathematical law has been established.

Any additional P40 Math-B-P7 primarily risks proliferating nearly equivalent source micro-courts rather than testing the **new central claim**: whether a minimal, source-calibrated *independent OO axiom basis* exists and where historically named SOLID principles can be derived without circularity.

## 4. P41 objective and falsification gates

**Principal research question:** Is there a *noncircular, irredundant, testable system of source–behavior–authority–dependency preconditions* that explains safe object-oriented evolution and supports exact **conditional** reconstructions of selected historical SOLID claims, while revealing their surviving classical equivalents?

- **P41-P0 — Canonical premises and target separation.** Fix syntax/typing, old-client observational equivalence, global objectives, edit authority, source boundary, and provider dependencies as different semantic sorts. Demand explicit contexts, observers, state/rights source and edit operators; forbid using `SafeGlue` as a premise disguised with new spelling.
- **P41-P1 — Independently observable premise contracts.** Source AST and control flow for source admission; native old/new clients for behavioral obligations; versioned authority/owner rights from verifiable provenance; separately measured dependency effects. Every premise gets a test oracle or a clearly declared synthetic status.
- **P41-P2 — Irredundancy court.** For each proposed axiom (A_i), construct a model satisfying all others and refuting (A_i), and a targeted safety proposition that fails under its removal. For real-source propositions, require a source-realized witness; synthetic-only claims must be marked.
- **P41-P3 — Conditional SOLID reconstruction.** SRP/OCP/LSP/ISP/DIP only as precisely scoped theorem families with typed contexts, observer sets and explicit permissible edits. Record failed implications and reduction to ordinary contextual refinement, frame logic, CSP, capabilities or rely/guarantee.
- **P41-P4 — Prospective cross-ecology falsification.** Pre-register real maintenance demands not yet known through experimental or issue-resolution outcomes; evaluate source admission, contract preservation and authority constraints as separate labels; run an independently formulated source-aware classical challenger with **identical evidence budget**.
- **P41-P5 — Scientific decision.** Publish either a legitimate additional constrained theorem with a genuine independently checkable distinction from classical rivals, OR a clear negative result that classical adequacy survives. No novelty promotion from terminology alone.

## 5. Acceptance, refusal and stopping rules

A P41 “PASS” cannot be inferred from P40's 16/16 or 6/6 selected source predictions. A new law claim requires: proved semantic premises, independent axiom nonredundancy, a noncircular target consequence, original-source realization of relevant counterexamples, prospective generalization, and serious source-aware classical theory competition.

A sound **negative** closure (e.g. the entire proposed basis reduces to classical contextual refinement + CSP + frame + authority capabilities) is an acceptable research outcome and must not be mistaken for task failure.
