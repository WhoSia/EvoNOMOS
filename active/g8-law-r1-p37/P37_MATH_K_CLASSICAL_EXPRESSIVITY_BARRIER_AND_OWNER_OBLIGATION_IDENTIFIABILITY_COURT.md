## I. What the scientific question can and cannot mean

EvoNOMOS wants an explanatory, falsifiable structural law of **software evolution**, explaining where SOLID-style recommendations apply, fail or reverse. The tempting demand that its repair reachability signature \(\sigma(H)\) differ from the *strongest existing theory* \(\sigma(B_*)\) is ambiguous. An unrestricted, exact classical labeled-transition-system model with all relevant inputs already computes the objective; a rival-discriminating test against **that** model cannot establish a second, conflicting correct reachability value for the same semantics.

Novel theorems about computational complexity, canonical representations, useful compressed invariants, practical learning under limited information, and real engineering causal effects are **not** excluded by this ceiling. What is excluded is selling a changed Boolean reachability label as a stronger correct prediction after the exact label has already been determined.

## II. K1 — exact classical expressibility of finite authorized repair

Let \(S\) be a source/deployment state universe, \(I\subseteq S\) the invariant-admissible checkpoints, \(G_d\subseteq S\) the future demand acceptance set, and \(\Gamma\subseteq S\times L\times S\) the owner/capability-labeled permitted edits. Define \(\operatorname{Pre}_\Gamma(X)=\{s:\exists \ell,t,\ s\xrightarrow{\ell}_\Gamma t,\ t\in X\}\). Put
\[
\Phi_d(X)=(I\cap G_d)\cup(I\cap\operatorname{Pre}_\Gamma(X)).
\]
Then for each \(s\in S\),
\[
\operatorname{May}_{\Gamma,I}(s,d)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s\in\mu X.\Phi_d(X).
\]

**Proof (no finite-state assumption required):** The iterates \(X_0=\varnothing\), \(X_{n+1}=\Phi_d(X_n)\) contain exactly states with a safe path of length **at most** \(n\) to a \(G_d\) state. The base includes zero-length paths in \(I\cap G_d\); induction adds exactly one safe permitted predecessor. Since \(\Phi_d\) preserves arbitrary increasing unions here (existential predecessor), the union of finite iterates is a least fixed point. Owner labels, capabilities and temporal obligations can all be included in the state and edge typing. In finite graphs termination is trivial. An unknown or unbounded source edit grammar is **not** thereby computable: exact semantics and extraction are assumed, not magically inferred.

**Full-information non-separation corollary:** If the comparator \(B_*\) has the *same correct exact* \((S,\Gamma,I,G_d)\), and \(H\) is sound and complete for the same May question, then \(\sigma(H)=\sigma(B_*)\) on every admitted input. Therefore \(\sigma(H)\ne\sigma(B_*)\) entails a mismatch in information, semantic assumptions, approximation, or at least one prediction error; it is **not by itself** evidence of a new law. This is a definitional representation/fixed-point consequence, not an impossibility result about future mathematical innovation.

## III. K2 — restricted information can be provably insufficient

Fix a comparison universe \(\Omega\) of source-tethered repair contexts, exact actual target \(y:\Omega\to\{0,1\}\), and a disclosed projection \(\pi:\Omega\to Z\) containing only information a comparator receives. A deterministic comparator \(b:Z\to\{0,1\}\) achieves zero error on \(\Omega\) **iff** \(y\) is constant on every fiber of \(\pi\):
\[
\ker\pi\subseteq\ker y.
\]
If there exist \(u,v\in\Omega\) with \(\pi(u)=\pi(v)\) but \(y(u)\ne y(v)\), **every** such comparator must fail on at least one of that pair. For a fixed finite labeled dataset, the minimum deterministic classification error given \(\pi\) is \(\sum_{z\in\pi(\Omega)}\min(n_z^0,n_z^1)\). This applies to any arbitrary nonlinear predictor *restricted to that information*, and is stronger than defeating one weak static metric; nevertheless it is ordinary kernel factorization and decision theory.

A richer owner/obligation model can predict correctly only because it sees variables deliberately unavailable to the restricted comparator; success must not be described as new mathematics beyond a full-information classical model.
