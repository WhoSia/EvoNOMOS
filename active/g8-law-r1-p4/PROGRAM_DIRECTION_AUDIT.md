# LAW-R1-P4 Program Direction Audit

EvoNOMOS has two coupled scientific ambitions.

1. **Axiomization audit.** Test whether named software-design principles—especially SOLID-family principles—are treated as stronger, more universal rules than empirical evidence warrants, including in LLM-mediated engineering where familiar principles may be reproduced as default advice.
2. **Conditional-law replacement.** Build a practical alternative in which design guidance is represented as observable context → rival structural interventions → non-collapsed lifecycle vector → falsifiers / sign reversals / abstention.

## P4 direction judgment

LAW-R1 is now substantively advancing ambition 2 rather than merely building research machinery. P0–P3 produced a concrete conditional mechanism with an explicit installation tax and a bounded support domain.

The remaining risk is asymmetric progress:

- ambition 2 has direct intervention evidence;
- ambition 1 is still mostly a motivating critique rather than an equally mature empirical program.

P4 therefore has two obligations:

- **do not broaden CIL by another favorable synthetic replay**;
- **establish both a transport-success cell and an abstention cell in real repositories.**

The program becomes a credible alternative to universal maxims only when the same law machinery can say both **TEST THIS RIVAL HERE** and **DO NOT APPLY THIS LAW HERE**.

## LLM-mediated engineering contact

The merged Uptime Kuma Indigo provider commit explicitly records Claude Opus 5 as a co-author. P4 treats this only as a descriptive witness that LLM-assisted contribution occurred inside the existing project architecture. It does not establish that the LLM caused the five-site membership path or that LLMs systematically over-apply SOLID.

Recent 2026 empirical work on LLM software design/refactoring motivates a future direct axis-1 experiment: agents can implement specified refactorings and patterns, but discovery of human-chosen higher-level refactorings/design rationale remains substantially less reliable than low-level/local transformations. That makes **principle-prior versus evidence-conditioned rival choice** a live EvoNOMOS target.

## Stop condition

If P4 cannot establish real-semantics transport **and** a real abstention boundary, CIL-C1 must remain at P3 authority and the program must not claim a general replacement framework.
