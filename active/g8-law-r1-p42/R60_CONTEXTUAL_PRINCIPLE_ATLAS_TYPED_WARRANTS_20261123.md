## A typed atlas
Context c=(D,T,G,A,J,E): change demand D, observable behavior/trace contract T, repair grammar G, legitimate policy authority A, cost or objective J, evidence E.
Three distinct claims: a repair preserves contract T; a team rule was legitimately enacted by authority A; a heuristic recommends an architecture under expected changes and costs J. These cannot be interchanged.
For fixed finite contexts C and principles P define evidence V_t:P×C→{support,defeat,undecided}. V_t is an adjudicated record, not an eternal truth predicate. In case support and defeat coexist without resolution, represent the record instead as a pair of bits (s,d)∈{0,1}², distinguishing neither, support only, defeat only, and both.
Support sets S+_t(p)={c∈C:V_t(p,c)=support} belong to the familiar powerset lattice (2^C,⊆,∩,∪). Importantly the observed collection of principle support sets need NOT be closed under meet or join. Inclusion never proves globally superior software design. The lattice is existing mathematics, not an original theorem.

## Constructive counterexample: costs reverse
Two candidate architectures M (future extension seam) and S (simple direct design). Toy lifecycle costs under demands d0=unneeded extension, d1=repeated extension:
| Demand | M | S |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| d0 | 4 | 1 |
| d1 | 2 | 6 |
If Pr(d1)=q then E[cost(M)]=4-2q and E[cost(S)]=1+5q; M is strictly less costly exactly when q>3/7, S for q<3/7. These are *chosen illustrative values*, not observations about actual OCP or YAGNI. The design recommendation reverses as the demand distribution changes; this is a standard decision-theoretic phenomenon and not a newly proved software law.

## Local charts and failed automatic gluing
Charts U and W may record different partial design judgments. Agreement of their local choices on U∩W is necessary for straightforward joining but does not alone guarantee that one global architecture satisfies cross-context system constraints. Any genuine gluing theorem requires a specified family of admissible solutions and verified restrictions. References to sheaves or category theory are analogies until their axioms and actual software semantics are demonstrated.
