Let \(N\) be a **finite condition/event net/system** with marking/case state, event alphabet, and an independently constructed reliance/dependence alphabet \(\mathcal C(N)=(E,I_N,D_N)\). A two-sided relation \(\mathcal R\subseteq S\times\mathrm{Markings}(N)\), rooted at initial pair, is an **annotated source/net admission certificate** only if it establishes all of:

## 3. P10-BRIDGE-1: classical trace-consistency admission theorem for future repair

Assume B1–B5, with \(N\) an actual C/E **system** meeting Aalbersberg–Rozenberg Theorem 5.39's setup. Let \(\equiv_{I_N}\) be the congruence generated by swapping adjacent letters whose source/net-corroborated system events are in the net-induced independent relation \(I_N\).

Then the legal source history language \(H_S\) is **\(I_N\)-saturated**: if \(w\in H_S\) and \(w\equiv_{I_N}v\), then \(v\in H_S\).

*Proof:* By bidirectional step-language correspondence, \(H_S=L(N)\). By original Theorem 5.39, \(L(N)\) is consistent with its induced reliance alphabet. By original Theorem 2.23, consistency is exactly closure under trace equivalence. Therefore \(H_S\) is saturated. **This is an application of two existing theorems plus a source/net proof obligation, not a new theorem of net theory.**

**IMPORTANT noncircular distinction:** \(I_N\) is derived from the net's pre/post conditions and cases; a source-only syntactic disjointness assertion is not yet \(I_N\), and an edit-owner's extra permission must be represented for \(H_S=L(N)\) to hold. An \(I_{client}\) derived only by seeing swapped terminal outputs equal is not an independent physical causal certificate.

## 4. P10-BRIDGE-2: conditions for quotienting new client claims and repair cost

Let \(K\subseteq E^*\) be one independently selected future client contract over **full histories**. Its eligible source repairs are \(G=H_S\cap K\). The *necessary and sufficient* language condition for representing its **binary success/failure** by the net trace quotient is:
\[
\forall w,v\in E^*,\quad w\equiv_{I_N}v \Longrightarrow
\big(1_{w\in G}=1_{v\in G}\big).
\]
By Theorem 2.23 this is exactly saturation of G. Since B1–B5 already give saturation of \(H_S\), saturation of K itself is an easy **sufficient**, potentially stronger, requirement. The relative condition on G can succeed even when K distinguishes histories that are impossible in H_S.

If the cost map \(c:H_S\to\mathbb N^d\) also satisfies \(w\equiv_{I_N}v \Rightarrow c(w)=c(v)\) and all declared reportable client-effect predicates are trace-consistent, then every eligible trace class has one unambiguous success judgment and exact cost vector. Hence, choosing **one canonical representative of each eligible trace** yields EXACTLY the same attainable cost set and Pareto-minimal vector repair frontier as enumerating all its legal sequential linearizations.

*Proof:* Bijection of the quotient's eligible classes with the partition of G, constancy of cost/predicates in each class, and set equality of cost vectors. No Pareto-frontier-set nesting claim is used. QED (ordinary quotient factorization).

A **minimal regular-DFA certificate** is available only after the relevant target language G is proved **regular**: original Theorem 3.35 checks for each DFA state p and each \((a,b)\in I_N\), \(\delta^*(p,ab)=\delta^*(p,ba)\). The criterion is for language consistency **given** I_N; it neither constructs a source-valid N nor authenticates owner rights. General context-free trace-language consistency is undecidable by the original theorem, so no universal repair-language checker can be promised.
