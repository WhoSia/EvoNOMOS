# P25 Mathematical Note — Boolean Bridge Replication and Decomposition Identifiability

Status: **PRE-FRESH / BINDING FOR P25**

## 1. Complete Boolean bridge packets
Let D:{0,1}²→{0,1} be a local decision map over transported quotient state q and an independent local gate g.
P24 froze and prospectively verified D(q,g)=q AND g.
P25 does not refit D. It asks whether the same extensional truth table transports to a second mechanistically distinct fresh family.

## 2. Proposition A — Complete 2×2 packet
A Boolean function on two Boolean inputs is completely determined by its four cell values D(0,0), D(0,1), D(1,0), D(1,1).
Therefore packet 0001 identifies AND extensionally; any single disagreeing cell falsifies AND for that packet once q and g are frozen.

## 3. Frozen rival ecology
AND=0001, OR=0111, XOR=0110, Q_ONLY=0011, G_ONLY=0101, NOR=1000, NAND=1110, XNOR=1001.

## 4. Proposition B — Interface equivalence does not identify architecture
If two mechanism families each export a binary q and share y=q AND g, equality of all q×g outputs does not identify whether they share one upstream transport architecture or merely share the same interface and local rule.
Thus shared bridge does not imply shared upstream architecture.

## 5. Proposition C — End-to-end evidence
For a frozen chain s → T(s) → Q(T(s))=q → D(q,g)=y, a held-out packet that contains distinct source states inducing q=0 and q=1 and crosses both with g=0/1 constrains the full composite, not only the final Boolean map.

## 6. Proposition D — Failure localization
With T,Q,D frozen independently, failed end-to-end prediction can be localized to transport, quotient, or decision layer by the first mismatching intermediate state. This is valid only when layer definitions are not edited after outcome.

## 7. Decomposition identifiability
U0: one common typed T→Q→D architecture spans families.
U1: distinct upstream families export the same binary q and share only D.
Action outputs alone cannot distinguish U0 from U1 when only q,g,y are observed.
A fresh end-to-end packet can support one real realization of U0, but U0 over U1 is not unique until a fresh prediction depends on an upstream restriction that U1 does not share.

## 8. Authority rules
P25 may promote second-domain AND replication, one new end-to-end T→Q→D realization, and bounded bridge replication.
P25 may not promote unique universal architecture, universal AND law, or shared transport algebra across unrelated mechanisms without direct upstream evidence.
