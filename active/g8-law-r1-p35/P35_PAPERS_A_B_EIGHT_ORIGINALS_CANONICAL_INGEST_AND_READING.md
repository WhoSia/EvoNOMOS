## 2. Paper A — what the actual originals establish, and what they DO NOT

**Huet (1980)** distinguishes an abstract reduction relation, local confluence and confluence, uses noetherian induction in relevant proofs, and considers critical pair overlap in specific term rewriting classes. This directly challenges the invalid analogy `textual git merge failure ⇒ software repair relation is nonconfluent`. A Go source tree, a Git hunk merge, and a semantic repair relation are DIFFERENT objects. Before invoking Newman's lemma/critical-pair arguments, one must state the exact relation, termination (if needed), admissible common reduct and equivalence of observable outcomes.

**Knuth & Bendix (1970)** attempts word-problem decision for particular algebraic identities using a well-order/decreasing rewrite rules and completion. Their procedure is conditional; it does not establish that arbitrary software edits have unique normal forms or that a failed automatic patch integration is a critical-pair nonjoinability witness.

**Gazzola et al. (2019)** separates generate-and-validate and semantics-driven repair. In its repair-generation section a solution to the approximating repair problem is described as **plausible**, with no general guarantee it fixes the original problem. Even original Go full module+raced acceptance PASS is a high-quality *test-backed witness*, not proof of full functional correctness outside the frozen oracle. The [Smith et al. 2015 APR overfitting original](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1C8y3I-lajj4bQy8zz_-molwA0DmTTHi7/view) remains an independent negative control.

## 3. Paper B — historiographic/agent-source critical reading

**Martin (2000)** explicitly limits the paper's architectural scope in its opening and diagnoses rigidity, fragility, immobility and viscosity as interrelated symptoms. His Open-Closed discussion refers to prior Bertrand Meyer; do not construe this paper as announcing a timeless five-axiom system. The origin of a modern acronym and its later institutional reception need independently dated primary evidence.

**Black (2013)** traces object-orientation to Dahl/Nygaard, distinguishes Simula I and Simula 67 and the later language/implementation traditions. Smalltalk's emphasis on virtual methods, dynamic binding and design choices is not equivalent to one unconditional SOLID prescription. **Kay (1993)** PDF is actually acquired and canonicalized, but its poor scanned text cannot support finely attributed historical quotations without visual review.

**Gloaguen et al. (2026)** primary arXiv v3 abstract reports repository-level context files did **not generally improve task success**, while adding **over 20% inference cost on average** in their tested agent/task settings, with instructions generally followed. Its limitation section says the study focuses on **Python**. Thus it supports separating instruction adherence from efficacy, but NOT an experimental claim that Go SOLID instructions generally harm agents, NOR a causal statement about training corpus contamination. Precise factorial Go coding-agent study remains prospective.

**Esposito et al. (2026)** multivocal review observes GenAI architecture studies often concentrate on early architecture lifecycle tasks, while rigorous testing of generated architectural outputs is often missing; also notes hallucination/precision and evaluation-framework challenges. This supports *testable empirical design*, not a claim that SOLID was taught as absolute fact or all coding agents are biased.

**Next Paper-B design:** freeze three equally visible instructions: (N) neutral coding guidance, (H) SOLID hard directives, (C) conditional SOLID guidance. Provide the same pinned Go tasks, tools and frozen feature tests to the independent agents, with randomized instruction assignment, hidden acceptance tests, and blinded structural/behavioral scoring. Determine whether hard directives actually increase architecture-forced code changes and whether success differs; do not infer training-weight mechanisms from prompt-only differences. Kay and Black historical analysis remains distinct from these modern coding-agent experiments.
