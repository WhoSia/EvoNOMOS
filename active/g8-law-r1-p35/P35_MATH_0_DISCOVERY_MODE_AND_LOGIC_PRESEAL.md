## Protected research question

Find and characterize **conditional laws of software structure and evolution**, including cases missed by SOLID, information hiding, caching, modularity and other established explanations. Theoretical terms must emerge from useful abstractions of real source counterexamples. Do not preselect *responsibility*, *dependency cuts*, *edit-site prediction*, *economic costs* or *SSA* as the object of the discipline.

## The mathematical research loop

1. **Problem notebook:** actual competing structures A/B, shared functional semantics, changing environments, unexplained outcome and strong classical rivals.
2. **Finite models and attacks:** Prolog and Go generate the smallest discriminating examples, classify universality/independence failures, and construct variants that contradict the conjecture. A finite search is not a proof of universal truth.
3. **Definition criticism:** make quantifiers, context universe, permissible changes, observations and total/partial operations explicit. Demand invariance when presentation changes; distinguish genuine theorems from restatements of definitions.
4. **Lean formalization:** state exact predicates, prove or disprove. Every theorem must be kernel checked without `sorry`, `admit` or unreviewed axioms. Audit statement-to-reality correspondence separately. Mathlib is introduced when a required mathematical structure benefits, not to decorate easy lemmas.
5. **Proof relevance:** test whether prior theory already explains the result. Only independently discriminating mathematics with real-Go contact may graduate from METHODS to CORE LAW CANDIDATE.
6. **Git checkpoints:** human WhoSia commits isolate conjecture, countermodel, formal statement, proof, repair and source-backed interpretation. A PASS that only means tools compile stays `INFRASTRUCTURE_PASS`.

## First intentionally modest mathematical specification

A world has `atConstruction` and `currentlyRegistered` Boolean bits; two implementation strategies observe either the current or the construction-time bit. This is an *abstract test harness inspired by*, not logically equivalent to, the full original Chi routing code.

`ObsEq(C, a, b) := ∀w ∈ C, observe(a,w)=observe(b,w)`.
An inclusion `C⊆D` yields `ObsEq(D) ⊆ ObsEq(C)` (context-refinement antitonicity). At construction-aligned worlds the strategies agree, but after flipping `currentlyRegistered` they disagree. This shows why equivalence at one observed environment does not imply equivalence under an enlarged world set. It is **elementary logic, not an original theorem**.

**Control negative:** `ObsEq({initial},live,snapshot) ⇒ ObsEq({updated},live,snapshot)` is false; Prolog and Go must both produce witnesses for both Boolean starts. Lean should prove the opposite with an explicit counterexample, rather than pretending that automation validates the false statement.

**Limitations:** Observation contexts here are explicit sets/lists of worlds, not an exhaustively specified language of all software contexts. This is not a minimal source repair theorem; not an experimental prediction of future source edits; not a measure of engineering cost. Counterexample search and proof checking have different truth tiers.

## Next genuine mathematical attacks

- **Observational quotients under demand extension:** distinguish equivalence under finite tests from contextual equivalence under arbitrary allowed command traces. Prove closure or produce countermodels for natural composition laws.
- **Composition and interference:** whether the preference order of independent architectures is preserved after product composition with an additional state machine. Define outcome order without forcing an arbitrary scalar cost.
- **Demand-family topology / order structure:** whether admissible-repair sets form monotone families and what counterexamples refute naive order monotonicity when structural constraints shift. Do not confuse existential repair witnesses with global optimality.
- **Non-SOLID theory competition:** embed classical information hiding, live-vs-cache and dynamic specialization as *rivals*. A conjecture only earns 'candidate' status if it differentiates at least one equal-information alternative and survives counterexample searches and an independent Go substrate.
